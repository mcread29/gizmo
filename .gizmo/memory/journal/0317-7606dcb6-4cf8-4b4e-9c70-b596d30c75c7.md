## assistant

Turns out there is a way — I had it slightly wrong earlier. Key points:

- **`/context`** — shows what's currently consuming space in your session (the closest thing to a real-time context breakdown).
- **`/usage`** — shows a "Behavior flags" section that surfaces things like "long context" when that behavior accounts for **10%+ of your recent usage**. That's almost certainly where you saw the "most of your usage is high-context sessions" message.
- There's no live percentage meter by default, but you can add one: configure your **status line** (`/statusline`) to continuously show context/cache usage.
- The other passive signal is the **auto-compaction warning** — when a session approaches the context limit, Claude Code tells you it's about to compact/summarize older messages.

So: no built-in live gauge out of the box, but `/context` gives you an on-demand snapshot, and a custom status line can make it continuous.
