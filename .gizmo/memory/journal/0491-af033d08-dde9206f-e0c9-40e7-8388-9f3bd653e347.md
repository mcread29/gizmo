## Why no migration is needed

I audited every place ids and filenames are consumed. Ids are **only ever string-compared** (`journal-store.ts:129` `.find()`, `resumeAfter`'s `Set.has`) and concatenated into a filename. Nothing does `parseInt`, regex-parses the ordinal, or infers the filename shape — including `backfill.ts`, which resumes purely on entry ids. So a bare `0310` and a suffixed `0451-deadbeef` are just two strings that each map to a file that exists.

The two formats also can't collide: `0451` and `0451-deadbeef` are distinct index keys *and* distinct filenames, so a new segment can never shadow a legacy one.

Migrating would actually be the worse option — it'd mean rewriting 450 files that the design (`journal-store.ts:47`) guarantees are written once and never rewritten, and it'd rewrite them differently on each machine.
