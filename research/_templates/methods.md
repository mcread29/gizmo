## Extraction and verification

- Pin the exact source artifact and hash it.
- Require a section/page/table/figure locator for extracted findings.
- Record whether the full text, an abstract, or only metadata was reviewed.
- Use a second reviewer for headline numerical claims and disputed extractions.
- Check publication status, corrections, retractions, and version changes.
- Keep positive, null, harmful, and qualifying observations as separate evidence records.

## Confidence rubric

Defer exclusively to [`../confidence-rubric.md`](../confidence-rubric.md). Record canonical claim type, relationship polarity, claim status, artifact-level maturity `M0–M5`, all `D/V/U/C/E/X/P/R` scores, deterministic observation grade, reasons for every 0/1, and all applicable caps. Do not combine these fields or define a topic-local profile grammar.
