## Why it matters

Influential memory stream using recency, importance, relevance, reflection, and planning.

## Methods and setting

25 GPT-3.5 agents in a two-day Smallville simulation. Human evaluation: 100 Prolific evaluators rank five conditions within subject; each sees one question from five categories. Ablations answer from memories generated by the full architecture's one simulation rather than independently simulated worlds. [evaluation section]

## Findings used in this library

TrueSkill μ/σ: full 29.89/.72; no reflection 26.88/.69; no reflection/planning 25.64/.68; crowdworker 22.95/.69; fully ablated 21.21/.70. Kruskal–Wallis H(4)=150.29, p<.001; pairwise tests mostly significant. [human-evaluation table] Descriptively, candidacy knowledge spread 1/25→8/25, party knowledge 1/25→13/25, and 5/12 invitees attended. Six of 453 relationship claims (1.3%) were hallucinated. [simulation analysis]

## Limitations / validity threats

One short simulation, no seed variation; thousands of dollars/multiple days but no exact cost. Ablations do not establish end-to-end trajectory causality. Failures include missed retrieval, embellishment, implausible location, and prompt/memory hacking.
