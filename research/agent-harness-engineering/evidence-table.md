## Evidence gaps

1. Few studies perform factorial experiments across model, context manager, controller, tool surface, memory, and verifier.
2. Long-horizon benchmarks rarely report enough repeated runs for strong uncertainty estimates.
3. Recovery, rollback, stale-memory invalidation, and permission systems have less controlled evidence than task success.
4. Multi-agent gains are often not compared with token/cost-matched single-agent baselines.
5. Self-modifying harness work needs held-out regression and safety evaluation.
6. Most direct “harness engineering” papers are 2026 preprints; peer-reviewed replication lags the terminology.
