# [HC029] Leases require resource-enforced fencing to reject stale writers.

- **Claim text:** Lease expiry at a coordinator does not stop a paused or partitioned former holder from later issuing an effect; stale-writer safety requires a monotonically increasing ownership epoch carried on every protected request and atomically rejected by the resource when older than its durable maximum.
- **Claim type:** synthesis
- **Polarity:** supports
- **Scope:** exclusive ownership of mutable resources under pause, delay, partition, and failover; all protected paths must participate
