# [HC030] Conditional writes prevent stale commits only at their atomic resource boundary.

- **Claim text:** Compare-and-swap, strong-version preconditions, and optimistic validation can prevent a stale writer from committing over a newer version, but conflicts cause abort/retry and the protection does not automatically compose across resources or undo an effect emitted before validation.
- **Claim type:** synthesis
- **Polarity:** supports
- **Scope:** linearizable conditional updates and optimistic validation over participating resources
- **Claim status:** supported
