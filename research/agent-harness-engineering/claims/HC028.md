# [HC028] Saga compensation is semantic repair, not isolation-preserving rollback.

- **Claim text:** A saga can compensate a committed prefix when suitable compensators exist, but intermediate effects may be visible, compensation can fail or be inexact, and irreversible actions cannot in general be restored to their prior state.
- **Claim type:** descriptive
- **Polarity:** qualifies
- **Scope:** long-lived multi-step workflows with separately committed subtransactions and application-defined compensators
