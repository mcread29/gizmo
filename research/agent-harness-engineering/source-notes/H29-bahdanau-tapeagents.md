## Findings used in this library

A structured append-only tape makes every thought/action/observation attributable and replayable and serves as resumable semantic state. The paper's strongest economics result combines tapes with supervised distillation: an 8B student scored 76.6% versus a 405B teacher's 75.8%, at reported $85 versus $28,157 per million turns. [Architecture §2; Tables 4–5; Figure 6]

## Limitations / validity threats

The cost result is not an isolated tape ablation; zero-shot 8B scored only 2.0%. The architecture is useful design evidence but remains a technical report.
