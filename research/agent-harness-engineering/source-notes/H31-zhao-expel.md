## Findings used in this library

ExpeL extracts reusable natural-language insights and retrieves successful trajectories. HotPotQA→FEVER transfer improved ReAct 63%→70%; ALFWorld scored 59.0% versus 40.0% for ReAct. Negative results matter: adding Reflexion text to insight extraction reduced HotPotQA 39%→29%, and random experience retrieval reduced ALFWorld 59.0%→42.5%. [Figure 5; Tables 1–3]

## Limitations / validity threats

Experience stores were modest; larger lifelong stores create another retrieval problem. Natural-language insights may inherit errors and require promotion/validation gates.
